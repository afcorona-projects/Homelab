## Likely cause

Likely the host pve2 experienced an out-of-memory condition that triggered the earlyoom.service to initiate a system shutdown, causing the orderly termination of containers like CT 133 and services such as postfix and alloy. The simultaneous exit of alloy nodes on both pve2 and komga indicates that the entire host machine was powered down or rebooted, leading to the loss of the /etc/aliases.db file for postfix during the abrupt stop.
